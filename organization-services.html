LEARN NOURISH REPEAT

More About Our Programming

Our services are educational in nature and begin in classroom settings. Through presentation elements and interactive learning we engage with participants to teach nutrition fundamentals and informed decision making. Course material is taught incrementally through simplified explanations, analogies, and comparisons, along with visuals and table games to empower learners of all levels. Participants are guided on how to shop nutritiously on a budget and are provided local resources in their area including food banks, meal services, and community pantries to alleviate potential obstacles towards accessing food. For organizations with adequate kitchen space suitable for hands on food-skills development, we offer expanded services beyond the classroom.
