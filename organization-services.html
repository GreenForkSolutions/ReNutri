Group Programming

ReNutri offers practical nutrition education and food-skills development for organizations serving their communities. Our services are designed to accentuate existing programming in places like treatment centers, hospitals, nonprofits, and government initiatives.

Contact Us Today ↗

More About Our Programming

Our services are educational in nature and begin in classroom settings. Through presentation elements and interactive engagement from participants, we teach nutrition fundamentals to improve informed decision making. Through this framework we also identify ways to optimize daily routines and improve healthy habits overall. Participants are also provided skills for shopping nutritiously on a budget and are provided local resources in their area including food banks, meal services, and community pantries to alleviate potential obstacles towards accessing food. For organizations with adequate kitchen space suitable for hands on food skills development, we offer expansive services beyond the classroom.
