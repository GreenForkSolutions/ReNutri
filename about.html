ABOUT RENUTRI

Recovery with nutrition. A personal beginning.

I founded ReNutri from a combination of professional experience in nutrition and my own lived experience with addiction and recovery.

I am a certified nutritionist and previously operated a business in the field. During the pandemic, my struggle with addiction became more serious. Over many years, I have learned a great deal through my own recovery journey.

Those experiences shaped my belief that practical food knowledge, accessible kitchen skills, and supportive education can be valuable parts of everyday life and recovery.

ReNutri is currently a founder-led initiative. My aim is to share approachable nutrition education and useful skills with individuals and organizations, while continuing to develop the programs and resources offered here.
